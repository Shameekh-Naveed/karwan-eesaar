# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mostly Pakistani donors and would-be volunteers on their phones, arriving from Instagram (@karwan_eesaar) or a WhatsApp forward. They skim, they don't read. Secondary: university students looking for internships, and people checking the organization is real before sending money.

Job to be done: in about five seconds, understand what Karwan-e-Eesaar does, see proof that it is real (photos, numbers, a real address and bank account), and act: donate, volunteer, or apply for an internship.

## Product Purpose

An introductory site for a small, volunteer-run welfare organization based in Mansehra (Old College Road). It exists to turn attention from social media into donations and sign-ups. Success is a visitor donating via Easypaisa / Meezan Bank / WhatsApp, or submitting the volunteer or internship form so the team can follow up.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, grounded. Faith-rooted without being preachy. Speaks plainly and briefly, like a neighbour asking for help, not an agency pitching. The Instagram presence sets the tone: deep navy and cream with a camel/gold accent, confident uppercase headlines, occasional Islamic architectural motifs and hadith.

## Anti-references

- Slick corporate NGO sites with stock photography and long annual-report prose.
- Generic template charity sites (teal gradient hero, icon-card grids, "Empowering communities" filler).
- Anything that feels like fundraising pressure or guilt imagery.
- Invented people, testimonials, or statistics. Everything shown must be real.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** A photo or a number beats a paragraph. Cut copy until it can be skimmed.
2. **Proof over promises.** Real photos, real figures, real account details; nothing placeholder ships.
3. **One obvious next step.** Every screen makes Donate (and secondarily Volunteer) unmissable.
4. **Match the Instagram.** The site should feel like the same organization a visitor just saw on their feed.
5. **Phone first.** Designed for a mid-range Android on mobile data before desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Large tap targets. Respect reduced motion (scrolling ticker and counters must stop). English-first, with the Urdu organization name kept in the logo; plain words for readers whose first language is not English.
